Keeping Your Indianapolis HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
