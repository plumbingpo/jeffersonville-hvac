Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Jeffersonville

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Jeffersonville HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Jeffersonville and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and every response is fully documented for your records.
